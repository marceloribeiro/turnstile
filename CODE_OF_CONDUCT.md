# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant**, version 2.1, as its code of
conduct. The full text is published at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: we are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity. We expect all participants —
contributors, maintainers, and community members — to act with respect and good
faith in all project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to
the project maintainer (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact options).
Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take appropriate
corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, in line with
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
